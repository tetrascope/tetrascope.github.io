## Data

The tables in `data/` are transcriptions of published analyses and of
positions read from published figures (Yoder & Tilley 1962; O'Hara 1968;
Tilley, Yoder & Schairer 1963, 1964). Their sources are cited in the matching
`*.provenance.json` files.
